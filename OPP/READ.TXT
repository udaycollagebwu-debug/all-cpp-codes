What is OPP ?
 --Object Oriented Programing  is a paradigm that organizes code around classes and objects, making programs 
   more modular, reusable, and easier to maintain. It is built on four main pillars: Encapsulation, Abstraction,
   Inheritance, and Polymorphism. 
OPP is define by two terms :
    1.Attributes
    2.Behaviors
